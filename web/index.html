You vs the model.
Every gameweek.

A statistical model predicts every match in the English top flight. You predict too. Every Monday you find out who was smarter.

A model that shows its working

Most prediction apps tell you who will win. Reckon tells you how confident it is — and then publishes whether it was right. Every gameweek, including the bad ones.

Honest about what it does not know

In the opening weeks of a season the model has almost no current data. It leans on last year, widens its uncertainty, and says so. Confidence is earned as the season is played, not claimed on day one.

No betting. Anywhere.

No odds, no wagering, no stakes, no links to bookmakers. Points have no monetary value. It is a game about being right, not about money.
