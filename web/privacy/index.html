Privacy Policy

Reckon · Last updated 12 August 2026

Reckon is a football prediction game. This policy explains exactly what data the app handles. It is short because the app collects very little — that is a design decision, not an omission.

What we do not collect

  • No email address. You can play without giving one.
  • No real name. Display names are generated by us, not entered by you.
  • No location data.
  • No contacts, photos, or device identifiers for advertising.
  • No advertising identifier (IDFA). We do not track you across other apps or websites, which is why Reckon never shows the "Ask App Not to Track" prompt.

Accounts

You can use Reckon without signing up. The app creates an anonymous account on your device so your predictions are saved. If you choose to sign in with Apple, we receive an identifier from Apple — we do not receive or store your email address or your name.

If you sign in with Apple and that Apple ID already has a Reckon account, the existing account takes over and any predictions made anonymously on this device are discarded. The app warns you before this happens.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account at any time from Settings → Delete account inside the app. This permanently removes your predictions, points, streak and display name. It cannot be undone, and we do not keep a copy.

Who we share data with

We do not sell your data. We use these service providers to run the app:

  • Supabase — database and accounts (hosted in the EU)
  • RevenueCat — subscription status
  • Apple — payments, and Sign in with Apple if you use it
  • Sentry — crash reports
  • PostHog — anonymous usage analytics (hosted in the EU)

Your rights

Under UK and EU data protection law you can ask for a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it. Deletion is available immediately in the app. For anything else, contact us at the address below and we will respond within 30 days.

Children

Reckon is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect data from them. The app contains no gambling, no wagering and no real-money features of any kind.

Changes

If this policy changes materially we will say so in the app before the change takes effect.
